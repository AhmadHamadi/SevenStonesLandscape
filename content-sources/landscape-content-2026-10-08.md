Research snapshot for four new pages. ## Page targeting

- `/blog/rain-garden-hamilton/`: regional informational planning, location-specific links to Hamilton/Green Venture and Halton programs, plus current program status; not a claim that Seven Stones offers a dedicated rain-garden installation service.
- `/blog/water-wise-landscaping-hamilton/`: regional water-saving lawn and garden practices with Burlington and Oakville rules; distinct from low-maintenance landscaping and sod-establishment content.
- `/solutions/muddy-side-yard/`: symptom diagnosis and choice of path/grade approach; avoids broad yard-drainage terms.
- `/solutions/runoff-eroding-garden-bed/`: localized runoff erosion symptom and soil stabilization; distinct from pooling/flooding pages.

The 2026 Rain Ready application status and deadline are date-sensitive and should be rechecked before future edits. Google guidance does not guarantee AI citations or visibility; these pages follow ordinary helpful-content and technical-search fundamentals.
